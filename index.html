Frequently Asked Questions

What makes TLA different from using a health app?
Most health apps track what happened. TLA is designed to help influence what happens next. Through a combination of personalised coach support, Atlas AI insights, structured accountability and regular review, the focus is not simply collecting data but helping you consistently follow through on the actions that matter most.
Is TLA Right for You?

TLA is for anyone with a health or energy goal who finds it hard to follow through. Whether you want to lose weight, get fitter, sleep better or simply feel more energised, we’ll build a plan around your life and help you stick to it.

It’s a good fit if you:

  • have a goal in mind, such as losing weight, building strength, having more energy or feeling better
  • know roughly what to do, but find it hard to do it consistently
  • want personal guidance, accountability and someone in your corner
  • can commit to a weekly session and a few minutes of daily routines

It’s probably not for you if you:

  • want medical diagnosis or treatment (please see your GP)
  • are looking for a quick fix or a crash diet
  • want a ready-made plan to follow on your own, without personal support
What happens on the call?
It's a relaxed conversation, not a sales pitch. We'll ask about your goals, your current routine and what's got in the way before. You can ask us anything, and we'll tell you honestly whether we think TLA is a good fit.

What is self-hypnosis, and is it right for me?

Self-hypnosis is a guided relaxation technique. You listen to a short audio session, usually while sitting or lying comfortably, and follow a calm voice that helps you slow down, settle your mind and focus on a positive intention, such as winding down in the evening or staying consistent with your routines.

It's nothing like stage hypnosis. You stay aware and in control the whole time, you can open your eyes and stop whenever you like, and nobody can make you do anything you don't want to.

Within TLA, self-hypnosis sits alongside breathwork and meditation as an optional part of your daily routine. Many people find these sessions a helpful way to relax, reset and build habits they can stick to. Experiences vary from person to person, and results aren't guaranteed.

Self-hypnosis isn't a substitute for medical or mental health care. If you have a diagnosed mental health condition, or you're unsure whether it's suitable, please speak to your GP or healthcare professional first. We're also happy to talk it through on your discovery call.

Is this safe for me, and is it medical advice?
TLA is a wellness coaching service. It isn't medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Before we begin, we'll ask a few health screening questions. Exercise and heat therapy such as steam rooms and saunas aren't suitable for everyone, so if you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, are pregnant, or take regular medication, please check with your GP first.

Programme Concept

TLA for teams · Launching 2027

Healthier, more energised teams, together

Over 12 weeks, each person works towards their own goals with their own plan, while the team supports each other along the way. No rankings, no comparisons, and individual health information stays private.

What we’re aiming for

  • More energy and focus
  • Healthier everyday habits
  • A more supportive team culture
  • Healthier ways of handling busy periods
  • Progress each person can see for themselves

Outcomes vary and aren’t guaranteed.

Who it’s for

Small teams, leadership groups, professional services firms, founder teams, and any organisation that wants to look after its people’s wellbeing together.

How It May Work

Week 1: Start together

Each person has a private starting-point conversation. The team agrees how it will support each other.

Weeks 2 to 11: Support each other

Everyone follows their own plan, with Atlas insights. Alongside it, you choose from team supports such as buddy check-ins, a short optional weekly team catch-up, and team habits like walking meetings or protected time for movement.

Week 12: Review and keep what works

Each person reviews their own progress privately, and the team talks about which habits to keep.

What stays private

Each person’s measurements, logs and conversations with their coach stay private. Your organisation never sees individual health information. Taking part is always voluntary, and nobody is ranked or compared. The only team-level view is an overall effort measure, such as the share of weekly commitments kept, which doesn’t identify anyone. Team-level results are shown only for groups of five or more, and only to the team itself.
