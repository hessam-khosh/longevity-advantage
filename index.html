1. Executive Summary: Looking Beyond Scale Weight

Standard bathroom scales treat every kilogram identically. Your Technogym Body Scan separates tissue quality from gross weight, providing an anatomical breakdown of cellular vitality, muscle density, and metabolic health.

2. Key Metric Breakdown & Strategic Guidance

• Cellular Vitality & Integrity (Phase Angle: 7° — EXCELLENT)

Phase angle measures electrical resistance across cell membranes. A score of 7° reflects exceptional cellular integrity, efficient mitochondrial energy transfer, and strong intracellular hydration. Strategic Focus: Preserve this high cellular quality while optimizing fat oxidation.

• Body Composition & Muscle Quality (SMM: 31.4 kg / BMC: 3.2 kg)

You possess a dense, metabolically active structural frame. The "HIGH" flag on total body weight (70.6 kg) is driven by muscle and bone density, not excess fat. Strategic Focus: Protecting muscle tissue while stripping body fat to lower total joint workload.

• Fat Mass & Metabolic Efficiency (BFM: 14.8 kg / 21% | BMR: 1,566 kcal)

Your current body fat percentage (21%) provides a clear, high-yield opportunity for targeted fat loss without risking lean tissue. The BMR reading (1,566 kcal) indicates an opportunity to up-regulate metabolic output through daily movement and non-stimulant vitality protocols.

• Cellular Hydration Ratio (ICW: 62.75% vs. ECW: 37.2%)

Over 62% of your body water is held inside your cells where energy is produced. This confirms strong hydration status and low systemic fluid retention outside the cell walls.

3. The 30-Day Directive Focus

  • Ignore Scale Weight: Success is measured by shifting body fat (21% → 17%) while preserving 31.4 kg of skeletal muscle mass.
  • Metabolic Priming: Activate non-exercise activity and low-glycemic fueling to shift your 1,566 kcal basal engine into a higher daily burn rate.
  • Mitochondrial Protection: Maintain your 7° Phase Angle through daily breathwork, hydration, and cellular recovery protocols.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Longevity Advantage?

Your Longevity Advantage is the benefit that comes from having greater energy, stronger health, sharper performance, and increased resilience than would otherwise be possible. It is the advantage that enables you to achieve more, enjoy more, and remain capable for longer.

What exactly does TLA offer?

The Longevity Advantage is a personalised vitality and longevity advisory service designed to help individuals improve their health, energy, resilience, performance, and long-term quality of life. Through tailored guidance, practical daily actions, and ongoing support, we help clients make meaningful, sustainable progress toward their goals.

Who is TLA designed for?

TLA is designed for professionals, business leaders, entrepreneurs, and individuals who want to take a more proactive approach to their health, vitality, and future wellbeing. Whether your goal is greater energy, improved fitness, healthy ageing, or enhanced performance, the guidance is tailored to your circumstances and objectives.

How personalised is the programme?

No two individuals are the same. Recommendations are tailored to your goals, current health status, lifestyle, preferences, and constraints. Rather than following a generic plan, you receive guidance designed around what is realistic, meaningful, and sustainable for you.

Do I need to be fit or healthy before I start?

Not at all. Many clients begin because they want more energy, better habits, improved fitness, weight management, or simply greater control over their long-term health. The programme is designed to meet you where you are today and help you move forward progressively.

Will I need to make major lifestyle changes?

No. The focus is on consistent, high-impact actions rather than dramatic overhauls. Small improvements, applied consistently, often create the greatest long-term results. We aim to build habits that complement your life rather than compete with it.

What makes TLA different from traditional coaching?

TLA combines evidence-informed longevity practices with highly personalised guidance, accountability, and practical implementation. Rather than providing information alone, we help clients translate knowledge into consistent action that fits their lifestyle.

How quickly will I notice results?

Every individual is different, but many clients begin noticing improvements in areas such as energy, sleep quality, focus, motivation, and wellbeing within the first few weeks. Longer-term benefits develop through consistent application of the programme's principles over time.

Is this focused solely on physical health?

No. While physical health is fundamental, true longevity also depends on mindset, resilience, purpose, relationships, recovery, and sustainable performance. The Longevity Advantage takes a holistic view of what it means to thrive throughout life.

What support will I receive?

Each client receives personalised guidance, regular review points, practical recommendations, and accountability support. The aim is to ensure you always know the next most important action to take and have support available on your journey.

How is progress measured?

Progress is measured through a combination of objective indicators and personal outcomes. Depending on your goals, this may include energy levels, fitness markers, body composition, recovery, habits, wellbeing, and overall quality of life.

Is TLA suitable for long-term support?

Yes. While some clients join to address a specific objective, many choose ongoing support as a trusted advisor for maintaining momentum, adapting priorities, and continuing to optimise their health and vitality over time.
